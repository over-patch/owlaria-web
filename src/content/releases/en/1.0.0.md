## Highlights

Owlaria brings your own digital comics together on Mac, iPhone, and iPad. Organize and read files from local folders and SMB NAS without changing your originals.

- Build a library from multiple local and NAS folders. Browse by cover or folder, search by title, author, series, and tags, and organize series and covers in the app.
- Read ZIP/CBZ, RAR/CBR, 7Z/CB7, LZH, and PDF files. Choose right-to-left, left-to-right, or vertical reading, with single pages, spreads, and continuous scrolling.
- Start reading ZIP/CBZ and PDF files from your NAS before the full download finishes. Download selected books in advance to read them offline.
- Pick up from your saved reading position and review your reading time, completed books, and pages read in Statistics.
- On Mac, open an individual archive or PDF in the Temporary Viewer without adding it to a library.
- Keep your source folders as they are: Owlaria does not rename, move, delete, or write management files to them. Organization data is stored in the app.
- Lock a private library in the app and unlock it with a password or biometric authentication on supported devices. This controls access through the app; it does not encrypt your source files.
- Use every feature for free with one library and up to 100 registered books across all data sources. A one-time Owlaria Plus purchase removes both limits. There is no subscription or time-limited trial.

## Before you start

- Bring your own files; books and comics are not included. Folders of loose images are not supported. PDF support is for viewing, without a table of contents, text search, or interactive links.
- Libraries, reading positions, and reading history are stored on each device and do not sync between devices. Reading the same NAS files on different devices does not sync your organization or progress.
- NAS access requires a reachable SMB 2 or SMB 3 server; SMB 1.0 is not supported. Save the books you need before going offline; a temporary cache is not a substitute for offline downloads.
- Owlaria Plus for macOS and iOS are sold separately. A purchase on one OS does not unlock the other. Restore purchases on the same OS using the same Apple Account.
